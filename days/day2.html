Day 2 · Sat 31 OctGola di Gorropu, and dinner at Su Gologone

The biggest hike of the trip, with Zain. Down from a mountain pass into one of Europe’s deepest canyons and back up. Then back to Cala Gonone, and the three of you go out for a big Barbagia dinner at Su Gologone. Zain sleeps in Cala Gonone and leaves for the airport before dawn.

Before you set off

  • Cash for the gorge: €6 each. It’s cash only and sold only at the kiosk at the gorge mouth (no advance booking).
  • Ankle boots. The boulders inside the gorge are polished white limestone, slippery even when dry.
  • 2 litres of water each. There’s one spring at the gorge mouth (marked by a Sardinian flag) to refill for the climb back.
  • Picnic lunch. If you want, the Hotel Gorropu at the pass (+39 329 337 6026) makes packed lunches when ordered the evening before.
  • Zain stays tonight. He leaves for the airport at about 03:15 tomorrow, so he doesn’t need his car today: take one car. Fill his tank this afternoon for the 230 km.
  • ✅ Su Gologone is booked: table for 3 at 20:00 in the Ristorante Tipico, under Aglinskas (confirmed 29 Oct). Phone +39 0784 287512 if you’re running late.
  • Buy Sunday’s Bue Marino tickets this afternoon (see Day 3). It can’t be booked online any more: the cave office says to buy at the kiosk in Cala Gonone port. 2 people, bring cash.
  • Shop for Sunday. 1 Nov is a public holiday and many shops will be shut or open only in the morning.

  2. 08:151Hike

    Down to the Gola di Gorropu

    One of the deepest canyons in Europe: white limestone walls up to about 500 m, closing in to a few metres wide. You walk down from the pass into the valley of the Rio Flumineddu, walk into the gorge, and climb back out.

    The trail, step by step

    Distance≈ 4.5 km each way
    Height≈ 600–650 m down, then up
    Time1 h 45 down · 2 h 15 up
    GradeE, steep in parts
    WaterSpring at the gorge mouth
    1. 08:15From the casa cantoniera take the path signed Gorropu on a wooden sign. Two paths rejoin after about 10 minutes. Waymarks are red-and-white paint and the odd green arrow. Don’t take shortcuts: it’s easy to lose the line on bare limestone.
    2. 08:45Down through holm oak, strawberry tree and juniper, past old stone sheepfolds. At about 2 km, Su Pinnettu. The views down the valley start to open up.
    3. 09:15Cross two scree fields below Costa Silana. Don’t stop on them (falling stones).
    4. 09:40A steep stretch on loose earth brings you down to the dry bed of the Rio Flumineddu. Weave between the boulders upstream for a few minutes.
    5. 10:00Ticket kiosk at the gorge mouth (40.18548, 9.50139), open daily 10:00–16:30 (a few sources say 10:30). €6, cash. Refill water at the spring.
    6. 10:00–11:15Inside the gorge. The first ≈ 500 m (marked green) is easy gravel and sand between huge boulders. The yellow section is a scramble over polished boulders, using hands, with some squeezes and fixed ropes. The hiking route ends at a big red dot on a giant boulder. Beyond it is for guided groups with ropes only.
    7. 11:15Lunch at the gorge mouth.
    8. 11:40Start back, at the latest, by the same path. The first 2 km out of the riverbed are the steepest.
    9. 13:45Back at the pass. Coffee at Bar Silana or the Hotel Gorropu.
    ≈ 9 km return+/− 650 m≈ 5 h 30 with the gorgeTicket €6, cash

  4. 15:002Shop

    Bue Marino tickets, shop for Sunday, rest

    First the port: buy two tickets for Sunday’s Bue Marino boat and cave at the ticket kiosk in Cala Gonone port (cash; the 15:00 boat if it runs). Ask which boats will really sail on Sunday and whether the sea looks OK. If they say 15:00 won’t run, take the 10:00 one and walk after it (see Day 3). Then shopping: Despar, Viale Bue Marino 16 (daily 07:00–20:30), or Europa Crai (daily 08:00–20:30). In Dorgali, Eurospin is open Sat 08:30–20:00. Hours come from OpenStreetMap and seasonal shops may already be shut. Buy picnic food for Sunday’s hike and Monday’s breakfast.

    Dinner at Su Gologone

    Classic Barbagia cooking by an open fire: roast suckling pig (porceddu), culurgiones, pane carasau and Cannonau. Its last weekend of the season. Booked: table for 3 at 20:00 in the Ristorante Tipico, under Aglinskas. Zain goes easy on the Cannonau: he drives at 03:15.

If things change

Getting Zain home

Decided: Zain stays for dinner and sleeps in Cala Gonone. He leaves by about 03:15 on Sunday, via Nuoro and the SS131: ≈ 230 km, about 3 h 30 in the dark. That gets his car back by about 06:45 for the 08:00 flight. Leaving at 03:45, as first planned, gets him there at about 07:15, which is too tight with the car return and security. He has sorted out the early drop with his rental company. The drive is on Day 3.

Running late

If you’re not at the gorge mouth by 10:30, go only as far as the end of the green section. The kiosk closes at 16:30 and everyone has to be out by 17:00.

Easier version

Swap with Sunday: walk Cala Fuili → Cala Luna today (less climbing). Or Tiscali from the Valle di Lanaittu (2–3 h), a village hidden inside a collapsed sinkhole.

If it rains

After heavy rain the Gorropu riverbed is dangerous: ask at the bar on the pass. Instead, the Grotta di Ispinigoli near Dorgali (a 38 m stalagmite; November tours at 11:00 and 12:00, +39 348 478 0104; the SS125 north of Dorgali towards Orosei is closed until March 2027, so phone to check the cave road) and a Cannonau tasting at Cantina Dorgali. Zain heads off after lunch.
