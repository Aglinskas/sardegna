Day 4 · Mon 2 NovSu Gologone spring, across the island, Capo San Marco at sunset

Mountains in the morning, the west coast in the afternoon. A blue spring at the foot of the Supramonte, two hours across the middle of the island, a rice-grain quartz beach, then a walk to the end of a cape to watch the sun set into the sea.

Before you set off

  • Check out of Cala Gonone. Fill the tank in Dorgali or Nuoro.
  • Mondays: the Cabras museum (since April 2026 it holds all the Mont’e Prama giants) and Tharros are both closed (winter hours from 1 Nov). Today is all outdoors.
  • Parking at Is Arutas and San Giovanni di Sinis is still paid on 2 Nov, the last day of the season. Install the ParkingMyCar app, or carry coins.
  • Head torches for the walk back from Capo San Marco after sunset.
  • A warm layer: the cape is exposed to the wind.

  2. 09:301Stop

    Su Gologone spring

    Sardinia’s largest karst spring: a deep blue-green crack at the foot of the Supramonte cliffs. Divers have gone down over 100 m and still not found the bottom. Walk about 300 m through the eucalyptus park, past the little church of Nostra Signora della Pietà, to the pool under the cliff. Toilets at the entrance.

    ≈ 45 min€2 entry (may be out of date)November hours: check

    Opening hours are listed as daily 09:00–20:00, but the date of that information is unknown. If the gate is shut, phone one of the numbers below.

  3. 10:15Drive

    Su Gologone → Cabras

    110 km · 2 h 15
    SP46SS129SS131 DCNSS131SS292
    1. Back to the SP46 and turn left into Oliena. At the roundabout go straight on, signed SP22 Nuoro, then left onto the SS129 into Nuoro.
    2. Through Nuoro follow Oristano / Sassari / Cagliari onto the SS131 DCN (Diramazione Centrale Nuorese). Stay on it for 50 km to where it joins the main SS131 near Abbasanta.
    3. Join the SS131 “Carlo Felice” southbound (towards Cagliari) for 24.5 km. Fuel: two Eni stations just after the junction (40.10937, 8.79296).
    4. Take the exit SS292 Oristano Nord / Siamaggiore. Through four roundabouts towards Oristano, then take the exit SP1 Cabras / Torregrande / San Giovanni di Sinis.
    5. After 2.3 km turn right onto the SP3 (Corso Italia) into Cabras.
    Speed checks

    No fixed speed cameras are mapped on this route, but mobile checks on the SS131 and the DCN are frequent. Limits change often: watch the signs.

  4. 12:302Lunch

    Lunch in Cabras

    Cabras is famous for muggine (grey mullet) from its lagoon and for bottarga, the cured roe. Grate it over spaghetti or eat it sliced with oil. On a Monday, try:

    • Zia Belledda, Via Amsicora 43 · +39 351 251 1104 · fish and local dishes, lunch from 13:00, closed Tuesdays
    • Ristorante Leopardi, Via G. Leopardi 53 · +39 389 762 0863 · uses certified Cabras bottarga, but on weekdays dinner only (closed Thursdays)
    • Trattoria da Attilio, Corso Italia 34 · +39 348 818 0811 · Mon–Sat 12:45–14:45

    Il Caminetto and Sa Bell’e Crabasa are closed on Mondays. Phone ahead: off-season hours change.

  6. 14:153Stop

    Is Arutas

    A beach made of polished quartz grains the size of rice, in white, pink and green. Taking any home is illegal and fined heavily, so photos only. Walk to both ends.

    Optional, if you’re ahead: Mari Ermi next door is wilder and its parking is already free (39.96937, 8.40044). Take the paved way (≈ 15 km, 30 min: SP59 → SP7 → Strada Comunale Mari Ermi), not the 3.5 km dirt track.

  8. 15:354Walk

    Capo San Marco: walk out to the cape for sunset

    A long, narrow cape with sea on both sides, the Punic-Roman city of Tharros on the east shore, two Spanish towers and a nuraghe on the high point. The sun sets straight into the open sea.

    The walk, step by step

    Distance≈ 5 km return
    Heightmax 56 m, gentle
    Time≈ 1 h 15 walking
    TerrainGravel track
    ShadeNone
    1. 15:35Walk south from the car park past the church of San Giovanni di Sinis, one of the oldest churches in Sardinia, and the little hamlet.
    2. 15:45At the Tharros entrance (closed on Mondays), keep right, outside the fence, on the gravel track along the west side of the site. You look down over the columns from the path.
    3. 15:55From 39.8714, 8.4395 a compacted gravel track (cars need a permit) runs about 1.6 km south over the cape. The Torre di Capo San Marco ruin is on the east side.
    4. 16:20Reach the high ground by the Nuraghe Baboi Cabitza (39.86313, 8.43591), about 2 km from the car. This is the sunset point: open sea to the west, Tharros and the Gulf of Oristano behind. The other viewpoint is at 39.86521, 8.43737.
    5. 16:30Optional: walk the last 300 m to the gate at 39.86024, 8.43451. The lighthouse beyond it is a Navy site, fenced and closed, but you can see it from the gate.
    6. 17:24Sunset. Wait 10 minutes for the afterglow.
    7. 17:35Head torches on and walk back the same way, 30–35 minutes. The cliffs have no railings, so stay on the track.
    8. 18:10Back at the car.

If things change

Running late

Skip Is Arutas and go straight to San Giovanni di Sinis by 16:00. Or skip the cape and watch the sunset from Is Arutas.

Easier or more scenic

Drive the slow, scenic way over Monte Ferru past the Sos Molinos waterfall instead of the SS131 (+30–40 min).

If it rains

Almost everything indoors is shut on Mondays, including the Cabras museum and Tharros. Before you leave the east, do the 11:00 tour at the Grotta di Ispinigoli (no closing day listed; phone +39 348 478 0104 to check the road), then drive to Oristano and wander its old centre and cathedral.
