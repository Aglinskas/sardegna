Day 8 · Fri 6 NovCosta Rei, Capo Ferrato and Villasimius

A slow last day of beaches and easy headland walks on the south-east corner: a wild granite cape, Costa Rei’s long white beach, a sandbar with sea on both sides, and flamingos below a Spanish tower at sunset.

Before you set off

  • Picnic and water for Capo Ferrato: there’s nothing open at the cape.
  • Swimsuits: Punta Molentis is the best swim of the trip’s last days.
  • Punta Molentis is free again. The 2026 ticketed, capped access ended on 31 Oct.
  • Pack tonight: you leave at 06:15 tomorrow. Put the car keys, passports and boarding passes by the door.

  4. 10:202Walk

    Capo Ferrato: coast walk to the lighthouse and the coves

    A wild granite and juniper headland at the north end of Costa Rei, with clear water and almost nobody around in November.

    The walk, step by step

    Distance≈ 8 km return
    HeightLow, small ups and downs
    Time≈ 2 h 45 + picnic
    TerrainDirt track, then rough path
    1. 10:20From the car park head down to the Porto Pirastu beach (39.29676, 9.61294), past the Nuraghe Porto Pirastu and a pine-wood picnic area.
    2. 10:35Follow the easy dirt track east, just above the sea, for about 3 km.
    3. 11:20Faro di Capo Ferrato (39.29934, 9.63356): an unmanned 11 m Navy lighthouse, gated.
    4. 11:30Past the Forestas barrier the track becomes a rough path with ups and downs, on to the coves of Portu s’Illixi and Cala Sa Figu. Picnic there.
    5. 12:30Walk back the same way.
    6. 13:30Back at the car.

    Alternative: trail 402 up Monte Ferru (300 m, about 5.5 km) past the Torre di Capo Ferrato and a Domus de Janas tomb, with views over Costa Rei and the Sette Fratelli. Waymarking isn’t confirmed: use OsmAnd or another OpenStreetMap app.

  10. 16:205Sunset

    Porto Giunco, the flamingo lagoon and the tower at sunset

    Behind the beach is the Stagno di Notteri, where flamingos winter: watch from the sand bar between the lagoon and the sea. Then walk 15–20 min up from the south end of the beach (or from Cava Usai) to the 16th-century Torre di Porto Giunco (39.11047, 9.52174) for the 360° view over both. Sunset ≈ 17:16.

    Or, for the sunset over the water: the Fortezza Vecchia viewpoints by the marina (free car park at 39.11567, 9.50558), 10 min away.

    Sunset 17:16
  11. 19:306Dinner

    Last dinner

    In Villasimius (see Day 7), or La Vela (Via degli Oleandri 12, +39 392 878 0232) or La Cala. Off-season opening isn’t confirmed for any of them, so phone ahead; Doppio Malto is the safest bet. (Mesa de Janas is a beach place at Notteri, probably shut.) Early night.

If things change

Inland instead: the Sette Fratelli

A forest day in the mountains with Sardinian deer. Drive 50 min to Campu Omu (park at the old cantoniera, 39.30894, 9.39979, or Caserma Umberto Noci, 39.30532, 9.39365). The CAI loop of Bruncu Perdu Cossu is 11.5 km and +550 m (≈ 4–5 h, trails 801 / 800B / 800). Shorter: trail 801 out and back to Arcu de Giulianu (≈ 3 h). Start by 11:00.

Or: Cortes Apertas in Mamoiada

The Barbagia food festival runs 6–8 Nov (confirmed in the official 2026 calendar), with courtyards opened for food, wine and music, and the Mamuthones mask museum. About 2 h 15 each way.

If it rains

Cagliari is an hour away: the Museo Archeologico Nazionale (partly closed for renovation and the giants are now in Cabras, so phone +39 070 655911), the Bastione di Saint Remy and the Castello quarter, then dinner in the Marina quarter. Park outside the ZTL.
