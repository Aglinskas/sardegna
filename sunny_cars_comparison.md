# Sunny Cars vs Avis at Cagliari (CAG)

Checked Thu 29 Oct 2026 on sunnycars.nl for Cagliari Airport, Fri 30 Oct 08:00 → Sat 7 Nov 07:30. Compared with the Avis + Chase Sapphire Preferred plan in [car_rental.html](car_rental.html). Euros converted at about $1.14.

## Verdict

**Sunny Cars is a good product, but not the right choice for this trip.**

- It costs about **$50–85 more** than Avis.
- The extra money mostly buys cover the Chase card already gives.
- **Every car is "Op aanvraag" (on request)**, so a booking made the day before pickup may not be confirmed in time.

Keep Avis. Sunny Cars only gets close if Veronica drives (the gap shrinks to about $20), or for a future trip booked a week or more ahead.

## What Sunny Cars includes

- **No excess at all** on damage (CDW) and theft (TP), including the radio and sat nav.
- **Glass, tyres, underbody** (including the oil sump and clutch) **and roof** covered. That matters on the Piscinas washboard road and the Goloritzé track.
- **A small card hold:** €100 on most cars, €300 on the Panda that includes an extra driver. Avis may hold €1,000–2,000 when you decline Super Cover.
- **No claim process.** With Avis, the desk charges the excess and Chase pays you back weeks later.
- **After-hours return included** (normally €30), so the 07:30 Saturday drop works.
- **Free cancellation until 1 hour before pickup.** Changes are free up to 24 hours before.
- Extra liability cover up to €10M, towing, lost keys and papers, unlimited km, and a full-to-full fuel policy.
- An extra driver costs €10.76/day (max €182.92) plus local tax, unless the offer already includes one.

## Why not for this trip

1. **Every car is "on request".** Sunny Cars first asks the local company, then emails a payment link. The booking only counts once the voucher arrives. Sunny Cars says last-minute bookings are often on request, and an old Sunny Cars blog post gives about two working days on average. Pickup is less than 24 hours away.
2. **You pay twice for the same cover.** Avis plus the Sapphire already gives near-zero excess risk. Sunny Cars' zero-excess package makes the Chase benefit largely redundant. The extra money buys convenience (small hold, no claim), not more protection.
3. **You don't know the rental company in advance.** Sunny Cars only shows "or similar" and a category code (for example YBH for the Panda). The local company is named on the voucher, so you can't check its desk hours or reputation first.
4. **The deposit card needs a PIN.** The terms ask for a credit card (Visa, Mastercard or Amex) with a PIN in the main driver's name. Debit, virtual and prepaid cards are refused. Check that the Sapphire has a PIN, or bring an EU credit card for the hold.

## If you want Sunny Cars anyway

1. Keep the free-cancellation Avis booking as a backup.
2. Book on sunnycars.nl and call **023-5699696** (open until 20:00). Ask them to push the confirmation, and ask which company and desk you'll get.
3. Cancel Avis **only after** the Sunny Cars voucher arrives.

## When Sunny Cars is the better pick

- Veronica will drive: about $20 more for no excess and a small hold.
- You'd rather not risk a Chase claim, or have €2,000 held on your card.
- A future trip booked a week or more ahead, when cars are more likely to be confirmed instantly.
