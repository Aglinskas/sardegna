Day by day

Times are a guide. Plan around daylight: sunrise is about 06:50–06:57, sunset 17:16–17:25, and it's dark about half an hour later.

Day 1 FridayWith Zain

Fri 30 Oct Up the Baunei coast: Pedra Longa, Golgo and Cala Goloritzé

The most spectacular coast in Sardinia, on the day you land. Up the fast new SS125 to the limestone coast of Baunei, a booked hike down to Cala Goloritzé (the trail closes to new walkers at 13:00), then over the Genna Silana pass to Cala Gonone after dark. You'll have been up since about 03:30, so the easy version is below.

Day 1 in detail: routes, parking, step by step →

  1. 07:201

    Land at Cagliari Elmas, pick up both cars

    Hand luggage only, so you should be driving by 08:00. Zain takes his own car (he needs it to get back on Saturday). Stock up on picnic food and water at the Conad in Quartu on the way out (08:25, open daily).

  2. 10:152

    Optional: Rocce Rosse at Arbatax

    Only if you’re past Muravera by about 09:45. Red porphyry towers right at the water's edge next to the port, a few minutes off the road. It adds 30–40 min and pushes the trailhead to about 12:40, so usually skip it.

    Optional · + 30–40 min
  3. 11:053

    Pedra Longa

    About 2 h 15 from Quartu, at the end of a steep lane below Baunei: a 128 m limestone needle rising out of the sea. Stay 15–20 minutes, then move on: the trailhead is the priority.

  4. 11:254

    Up to the Golgo plateau

    Fill up at the Api-IP in Baunei (the last fuel before Dorgali), then 40 min up the hairpins to a wild limestone plateau with free-roaming pigs, goats and donkeys. Park at Su Porteddu, the Goloritzé trailhead, by about 12:05. After the hike, Su Sterru, a near-perfect 270 m deep shaft, is five minutes away for the sunset.

  5. 12:055

    Hike to Cala Goloritzé

    Show your QR code at the kiosk. The path drops through a limestone canyon to the beach with the 143 m Aguglia spire and the natural arch. Picnic and swim at the bottom, leave the beach by 14:45, and you’re back up at about 16:45. Booking is mandatory until 15 Nov (€7, opens at 00:00 on Tue 27 Oct, 250 people at a time). The trail closes to new walkers at 13:00 and the beach at 16:00.

    ≈ 3.5 km each way1 h 15 down, 2 h up≈ 500 mBooked ticket + closed shoes
  6. 17:156

    SS125 over the Genna Silana pass

    Sunset at Su Sterru (17:21), then back down through Baunei and north on the old SS125 over the 1,017 m pass, one of the great mountain roads of Italy. You drive it in the dark: slowly, with animals on the road and no fuel. You cross the trailhead of tomorrow's hike at the top and see it all in daylight in the morning.

    ≈ 1 h 35, after dark
  7. 18:507

    Down the hairpins to Cala Gonone

    Check in for three nights. Most of Cala Gonone is shut for the winter, so phone ahead for dinner or cook.

Alternative / easier

Skip the Goloritzé hike: walk around the Golgo plateau (Su Sterru, the San Pietro church, the Piscinas rock pools) and cross Genna Silana in daylight to be in Cala Gonone by about 16:30 for a nap.

If it rains

Drive the inland route via the SS131 and Nuoro instead (2 h 30), with the Museo del Costume or the MAN in Nuoro, then the Grotta di Ispinigoli near Dorgali. Do Goloritzé on Sunday instead of Cala Luna (it needs a new booking).

Day 2 SaturdayWith Zain · his last day

Sat 31 Oct Gola di Gorropu, then Zain drives back

The big hike, with Zain. One of Europe's deepest canyons, with walls up to about 500 m and a river bed full of white boulders. The trailhead is on the SS125 heading south, so Zain packs his car in the morning and drives to Cagliari straight from the pass.

Day 2 in detail: routes, parking, step by step →

  1. 07:301

    Both cars up the SS125 to Passo di Genna Silana

    40 min of mountain road to the 1,017 m pass. Zain checks out and brings his bag. Park by the old cantoniera and Bar Silana. Bring lunch, 2 litres of water each and €6 cash each.

  2. 08:152

    Hike down into the Flumineddu valley and the gorge

    A well-trodden path drops about 600 m through holm oak and juniper, about 1 h 45 down. Buy the entry ticket (€6, cash only) at the kiosk at the gorge mouth, open daily 10:00–16:30. Walk into the narrows as far as the big red dot on a giant boulder. Beyond it is for guided groups with ropes.

    ≈ 9 km return≈ 5 h 30+/− 650 m€6 cash
  3. 11:401

    Climb back out

    Start back by 11:40 at the latest: about 2 h 15 up to the pass. The view down the valley is the reward. Coffee at Bar Silana or the Hotel Gorropu.

  4. 14:00

    Zain: Genna Silana → Cagliari

    About 3 h south on the SS125 via Baunei and Tortolì, then the new SS125, so he reaches the airport around 17:15, at sunset. Dinner in the Marina quarter, a night near the airport, then his 08:00 flight on Sunday.

    ≈ 170 km≈ 3 h
  5. 14:003

    You: back to Cala Gonone

    40 min back down. Shop for Sunday (it’s a public holiday), then a sunset walk along the seafront to the little Palmasera beach (sunset ≈ 17:21).

  6. 20:004

    Dinner at Su Gologone

    Classic Barbagia cooking: roast suckling pig, culurgiones, pane carasau and Cannonau wine. 40 min from Cala Gonone. It’s the last weekend of the season: book now by phone or WhatsApp (+39 0784 287512). Fallback: Il Colibrì in Dorgali.

Getting Zain home

Option A (recommended): Zain drives back on Saturday afternoon, straight from the trailhead, and sleeps near the airport. About 3 h, mostly in daylight, and an easy morning before the 08:00 flight. Option B: Zain stays for the Su Gologone dinner and leaves Cala Gonone at about 03:45 on Sunday (≈ 3 h 15 via Nuoro and the SS131), returning the car by about 07:00. Possible, but brutal after a 6-hour hike. Either way, check the car-rental return hours at CAG (or use the key box).

Alternative / easier

Cala Fuili → Cala Luna (today's and tomorrow's hikes swap places): less climbing, more sea, and Zain can still leave Cala Gonone by 14:30 (≈ 3 h 15 via Nuoro). Or Tiscali from the Valle di Lanaittu: a 2–3 h hike up to a collapsed sinkhole with a hidden village inside.

If it rains

After heavy rain the Gorropu riverbed is dangerous, so ask at the pass. Do the Grotta di Ispinigoli instead (a 38 m stalagmite; November tours at 11:00 and 12:00; phone +39 348 478 0104 first, as the SS125 north of Dorgali is closed) and a Cannonau tasting at Cantina Dorgali. Zain can head off after lunch.

Day 3 Sunday · holiday

Sun 1 Nov Cala Luna on foot, and the Bue Marino cave

A coastal walk that's a boat trip in summer. In November you'll have Cala Luna almost to yourselves. All Saints' Day is a public holiday, so shops are shut: buy picnic food on Saturday.

Day 3 in detail: routes, parking, step by step →

  1. 07:451

    Cala Fuili

    Ten minutes south of Cala Gonone, at the end of the road. A narrow pebble cove at the mouth of a gorge, about 100 steps down from the car park.

  2. 08:002

    Coastal path to Cala Luna

    Cliffs, juniper and big sea views, on a rocky path that goes up and down the whole way. Cala Luna is a crescent beach backed by six huge caves and a small lagoon with oleanders.

    ≈ 5 km each way≈ 2 h each wayRocky
  3. 10:002

    Lunch inside the caves

    Caves, a swim and an early lunch in the shade. Bring everything: the beach kiosks are shut for the season. Start back by 11:30 to be at Cala Fuili about 13:30.

  4. 15:003

    Boat to the Grotta del Bue Marino

    The sea cave where monk seals once lived, with an underground lake and stalactites. It’s reached only by boat: on 1 Nov they leave Cala Gonone port at 10:00, 11:00 and 15:00 (later in November on request only). Book the 15:00 boat + cave ticket (≈ €40) at grottabuemarino.it or on +39 338 834 1618. Calm sea only.

    Book ahead≈ 1 h 45
Alternative / easier

Walk only as far as the first big viewpoint (about 45 min) and come back for an afternoon by the sea in Cala Gonone. The boats from Cala Gonone port run until 1 Nov but need 10 passengers: if one sails (+39 0784 93305), ride the 12:30 boat back from Cala Luna instead of walking.

If it rains

Grotta di Ispinigoli (if not done yet; phone first) and a Cannonau tasting at Cantina Dorgali. Or drive to Orgosolo for its political murals (45 min).

Day 4 Monday

Mon 2 Nov Su Gologone spring, across the island, Capo San Marco at sunset

Morning in the mountains, afternoon on the west coast, and the sun sets into the sea at the end of the cape. Tharros is no longer a stop: you walk past it on the way out and see it from the path.

Day 4 in detail: routes, parking, step by step →

  1. 09:301

    Su Gologone spring

    Sardinia's largest karst spring: a deep blue-green crack at the foot of the Supramonte cliffs, with divers yet to find its bottom. Short walk, small entry fee. November hours aren’t published, so phone first (+39 328 564 9983).

    ≈ 45 minHours: phone
  2. 10:152

    Across the island

    About 2 h 15 via Oliena, Nuoro and the SS131. Lunch in Cabras: it's famous for muggine (grey mullet) and its bottarga. Zia Belledda opens at 13:00.

  3. 14:153

    Is Arutas and Mari Ermi

    Beaches made of polished rice-grain quartz pebbles, white, pink and green. Taking any home is illegal (fines are big), so photos only. Mari Ermi, next door, is wilder. Parking is still paid today, the last day of the season: ParkingMyCar app or coins.

  4. 15:354

    Capo San Marco headland walk

    Park near San Giovanni di Sinis. Walk out along the narrow cape, past the edge of Tharros (closed on Mondays) and the Spanish tower, to the high ground by the nuraghe near the tip, with sea on both sides. The lighthouse beyond is a fenced Navy site. Time it so you're on the cliffs for sunset, and bring a head torch for the walk back.

    ≈ 5 km round trip≈ 1 h 15 walkingEasySunset ≈ 17:24
  5. 18:152

    Check in at Cabras

    25 min back to town. Dinner at Zia Belledda (from 20:00) or Leopardi (from 19:45).

Alternative / easier

Skip Capo San Marco and watch the sunset from Is Arutas instead. Or drive the scenic way past the Sos Molinos waterfall (Monte Ferru) instead of the SS131.

If it rains

The Cabras museum (now home to all the Mont'e Prama giants) and Tharros are closed on Mondays. Do the 11:00 tour at Ispinigoli before you leave the east, then wander Oristano’s old centre.

Day 5 Tuesday

Tue 3 Nov Piscinas dunes, Buggerru and Porto Flavia

Down the Costa Verde to the mining coast. Desert dunes in the morning, a tunnel through a cliff in the afternoon, and the best sunset spot of the trip to finish.

Day 5 in detail: routes, parking, step by step →

  1. 10:151

    Dune di Piscinas

    About 2 h south via Guspini, Arbus and the abandoned Ingurtosu mining valley; the last 5 km is washboard dirt road. Come in this way: the northern approach has river fords. Some of the tallest dunes in Europe, rolling back from a 7 km wild beach, with no military zone and nobody there in November. Climb the big ones for the view down the Costa Verde.

    Free≈ 2 h from CabrasLast stretch is a dirt road
  2. 13:002

    Lunch in Buggerru

    About 1 h 10: back up the dirt road and round by the SS126, as there’s no coast road south of Piscinas. Coffee and lunch by the tiny harbour; phone first, as November hours aren’t confirmed.

  3. 15:003

    Porto Flavia

    A 1920s mining port tunnelled straight through the cliff, opening onto the sea directly facing Pan di Zucchero. Guided visits only (€13, ≈ 50 min, closed shoes). No November timetable has been published, so phone IAT Iglesias (+39 0781 274507) this week to confirm a tour on Tue 3 or Wed 4 Nov, then book at iglesiasturismo.it. If there’s none, swap it for Pan di Zucchero from Masua beach.

    ≈ 50 min€13November tours: confirm
  4. 16:404

    Sunset at the Nebida belvedere

    A railed walk above the sea, looking down on the ruined Laveria Lamarmora ore-washing plant and across to Pan di Zucchero. The laveria and the stairway down to it are closed for safety works, so watch from the south viewpoint. Probably the best sunset spot of the trip.

    Sunset ≈ 17:23Laveria closed
Alternative / easier

Skip Piscinas and take the SS131 / SS130 straight to Iglesias, then do Porto Flavia and a lazy afternoon at Masua.

If it rains

Porto Flavia is inside a tunnel, so keep it. Swap Piscinas for a Montevecchio mine tour (open on Tuesdays by booking, +39 338 459 2082). The Su Mannau caves are closed from November.

Day 6 Wednesday

Wed 4 Nov Hike day: the cliffs of Cala Domestica, and the Grotta Azzurra

The Saturday hike from version 1, now midweek. Everything is within 25 minutes of the bed.

Day 6 in detail: routes, parking, step by step →

  1. 09:001

    Cala Domestica – Buggerru – Pranu Sartu loop (trail 301)

    A fjord-like cove between limestone cliffs, guarded by a Spanish tower. Trail 301 goes inland over the Genna Arenas saddle to Buggerru (coffee), then back along wild cliff tops past the abandoned Pranu Sartu mine, down an easy rock step into the hidden cove of La Caletta, and through a short mine tunnel onto the beach. The cliff section is marked only with cairns, so carry the GPX, and don’t do the rock step if it’s wet.

    ≈ 11.5 km loop4 h 30–5 h+350–500 mNo shade
  2. 13:301

    Picnic and (maybe) a swim at Cala Domestica

    The sea is still about 20 °C. The cove is sheltered from everything except a strong westerly. Bring lunch: the beach bar is probably shut.

  3. 15:152

    Boat to the Grotta Azzurra, or Pan di Zucchero from Masua

    A small sea cave about 1 km north-west of Pan di Zucchero that glows electric blue inside. You can only get there by boat, RIB or SUP from Masua beach, and only in calm sea. The Masua operators run April–October, so a November trip is unlikely: ask Warung Beach (+39 392 819 7964) about a private trip. If nothing runs, sit on Masua beach under the sea stack.

    Unlikely in NovemberSea-state dependent
  4. 16:50

    Sunset at Nebida or Masua

    Back to the Nebida belvedere for a second round (sunset ≈ 17:22), or watch from Masua beach behind Pan di Zucchero. The path to the Spanish tower above Cala Domestica is overgrown.

Alternative / easier

The short CAI loop (≈ 7 km, +400 m) skips Buggerru: inland track to Pranu Sartu, then the cliffs back to La Caletta. Or the easy Cammino di Santa Barbara path from Nebida to Masua (≈ 6 km one way, about 2 h): walk out as far as you like and turn round.

If it rains

The caves are out: Su Mannau is closed from November and Is Zuddas only opens at weekends. Instead, a Montevecchio mine tour (by booking, +39 338 459 2082). Do the Porto Flavia tunnel tour today if you missed it yesterday. Coffee in Iglesias old town.

Day 7 Thursday

Thu 5 Nov Porto Pino, the Costa del Sud, Chia and the Sella del Diavolo

V1's landing day, run backwards. The whole south coast in one go, ending with a sunset walk above Cagliari and a short drive to the last base.

    Porto Pino beach and dunes

    Long white beach, pine forest and pink-tinged lagoons. Walk east along the beach towards the famous white dunes (Sabbie Bianche), as far as the military boundary signs: the dunes are inside the Capo Teulada firing range and only open in summer.

    1 h 10 from NebidaWhite dunes closed

  3. 12:053

  4. 15:354

    The headland at the end of Poetto beach: a short climb on a rocky path to old WWII lookouts, with views down onto turquoise coves and across the whole Gulf of Cagliari. Park at Marina Piccola, be on top for sunset, and bring head torches for the way down.

For which company and cover to pick, see the car rental guide.
  • Book the 4 stays (above), plus Zain's Fri night in Cala Gonone and his Sat night near the airport. Cancellable options are best in November.
  • Book Cala Goloritzé in the “Heart of Sardinia” app or heartofsardinia.com at 00:00 on Tue 27 Oct, i.e. Monday night (€7 each, 250 people at a time, mandatory until 15 Nov). If offered, take the afternoon access (enter 12:00–13:30). Bring closed shoes.
  • Book dinner at Su Gologone for Sat 31 Oct (+39 0784 287512). It’s the last weekend of its season, so book now by phone or WhatsApp; fallback Il Colibrì, Dorgali (+39 340 721 1564).
  • Book the Bue Marino boat for Sun 1 Nov at 15:00: grottabuemarino.it (boat + cave ticket) or +39 338 834 1618.
  • Check your rental’s early-return procedure at CAG for Sat 7 Nov (key box before 08:00). Avis/Budget have a 24 h key box; Sixt takes keys at its check-in cabin; Hertz, Europcar and Goldcar list no key box.
  • Porto Flavia: no November timetable is published, so phone or email IAT Iglesias (+39 0781 274507, infoturistiche@comune.iglesias.ca.it) to confirm a tour on Tue 3 or Wed 4 Nov, then book at iglesiasturismo.it.
  • Ask Warung Beach (+39 392 819 7964) about a private Grotta Azzurra boat on Wed 4 Nov. Expect a no: the Masua operators run April–October.
  • Phone the Su Gologone spring (+39 328 564 9983) to check it opens on Mon 2 Nov morning.
  • Strike check: a general strike is called for Fri 30 Oct. Air transport is in a protected period (30 Oct – 5 Nov), so your early flight should be fine, but check the Ryanair app the night before.
  • Download offline maps and GPX tracks: Su Porteddu – Goloritzé, Genna Silana – Gorropu, Cala Fuili – Cala Luna, trail 301 Buggerru loop (Cala Domestica).
  • Buy picnic food on Sat 31 Oct: Sun 1 Nov is a public holiday.
  • Check the wind forecast each evening: when the maestrale (NW) blows, the west is rough and the east coast calm. If it blows hard on Wed 4 Nov, the Grotta Azzurra boat won't run.

Good to know in early November

  • Weather: usually 18–22 °C and sunny spells, sea about 20 °C. The forecast on 26 Oct shows a dry week, with a fresh northerly on the east coast on 1–2 and 6–7 Nov; it’s long-range, so check again. Pick each day's coast by the wind.
  • Off-season: many beach bars and resort restaurants are shut. Towns (Iglesias, Cabras, Dorgali, Villasimius) still have places open. Carry water and picnic food on hike days.
  • Sun 1 Nov is a public holiday (Ognissanti): shops shut, buy food on Saturday. Mondays are museum closing days.
  • Festival: Autunno in Barbagia / Cortes Apertas is in Desulo 31 Oct–1 Nov, and Mamoiada 6–8 Nov (Ovodda 7–8 Nov).
  • Roads: the SS125 between Dorgali and Orosei is closed until March 2027. It isn’t on the route.
