Before you book. Prices are live and will move. Booking.com totals usually exclude the local tourist tax (a euro or two per person per night, paid on arrival, and often waived out of season). November is the very end of the season: message each host with your arrival time when you book, since every night you arrive after dark.

Range

What matters here

  • Two bedrooms, or a bedroom plus a sofa bed, so Zain has his own bed on Friday. Searched for 3 adults for all 3 nights, so the price already covers him.
  • You arrive at about 18:50, so the host needs to be OK with that, or offer self check-in.
  • Parking for two cars on Friday night (Zain has his own car).
  • A kitchen helps. Most of Cala Gonone is shut in November, and Sun 1 Nov is a public holiday.

My pick

Appartamento a 50 mt dal mare or Apartment Altomare. They're on the same street, 2-bed, rated 9.2–9.3 and €105–110 a night all in, two minutes' walk from the seafront. For more comfort, go for the Penthouse, the only one with heating, two bathrooms and a sea-view veranda.

Plan B: separate rooms

If you'd rather not share a flat with Zain, book a double for the two of you for 3 nights and a single room for him on Friday. B&B AcquaDolce (9.3, €270 for 3 nights) plus Affittacamere Marizzola for Zain (9.2, €77.50) or Hotel La Playa (8.8, €89.50 with breakfast) comes to about €348–360.
